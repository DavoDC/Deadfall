# What's in the jungle

- **Geography is the antagonist.** THE PALISADE - an unclimbable mountain wall - crosses the south, pierced only at the Howling Gate, Rivergate Canyon and the Skyreach Saddle. The flooded gorge cuts the middle (three sills; razorfins eat swimmers). THE FRACTURE steps the north up 9m of cliff, passable at Bonebridge (a colossal ribcage) or the Scree. Walls of strangler thicket stagger every lane. The straight line is not risky - it is physically impossible, and that is a unit test.
- **THE MIREMOTHER.** The extraction butte is ringed by cliffs; its single drowned stair climbs out of the Sunken Maw - the flooded moat where the apex serpent waits. Her hood opens before she strikes: the tell is the crit window. At 66% and 33% she dives, and a foam wake hunts you. Kill her for a feast, or sprint the stair past her and live with the venom.
- **Live weather that plays.** Storms swallow your gunfire (the director barely hears shots under thunder), fog banks ruin the hunters' tracking, lightning walks the canopy. Weather rolls DURING the run.
- **A deceptive jungle.** Viperblossoms - flowers until you stand next to them. Snapjaw bone traps seeded beside tempting loot. Dart totems wired across the very corridors the geography forces you through. Trust nothing pretty.
- **Six hunters + the fauna's tells:** Prowlers flank in pairs, Spitters SWELL as you wound them, Stingerwing swarms, Canopy Shrikes (dangerous only airborne), Bloomcallers scream reinforcements in, the Juggernaut's bone plate deflects - shoot the boil on its back. Orange hitmarkers mean you found a weakpoint.
- **Every hunter on the way north.** On every difficulty the route is authored with encounter slots (`app/src/game/routeEncounters.js`): the first contact comes inside the first tenth of the walk, then a new group roughly every tenth, so Prowlers, Stingerwings, Spitters, a Bloomcaller, a Shrike and a Juggernaut are all met well before the Maw, with Viperblossoms planted along the way. RECRUIT keeps the same variety in smaller groups; DEADFALL brings more of them. Shortly before the drowned stair something huge answers from the Maw, so the apex is never a surprise.
- **A threat director**, not spawn timers - escalates with time, noise and PROGRESS; garrisons whichever gate, sill or way you approach; throttles itself during the boss fight so the fight IS the fight; and answers your extraction call with waves pouring up the stair.
- **An arsenal with opinions, held by alien hands.** Auto carbine, pump breacher (watch the off-hand work the pump), and the LONGARM - carried empty; its ammo is loot, and it auto-chambers the first find. Real ADS, frag grenades with an arc preview, a machete that never runs dry.
- **Ayahuasca is a real trip.** Drink the vine: the world warps and breathes, the jungle writhes, and every creature GLOWS THROUGH the foliage - x-ray perception, 25 seconds, on top of speed and healing.
- **Named ground.** The Rapids, Cinder Fang, Bonebridge, the Sunken Maw - location reveals as you discover them, and an ember-tipped spire you can navigate by from anywhere in the north.
- **The birds know first.** Birdsong thins as something closes in; below it a drone swells. The silence is the warning.

## Original pitch

The first README intro, kept verbatim:

One run, one question: which way north? Insert at the southern treeline and pick your way through a world that is all walls and choices - a mountain Palisade with three gates, a flooded razorfin gorge with three sills, a highlands cliff with two ways up - while storms, fog banks and the threat director hunt you. At the end, a flooded moat, the thing that lives in it, and one drowned stair to the extraction beacon. Die or extract - either way the run is a complete story.
- **A controls reminder.** On a fresh profile a one-time overlay lists every bound control (wheel weapon switch, V melee, reload, sprint, jump, pause); the menu's CONTROLS button reopens it (`app/src/player/controlsOverlay.js`).
